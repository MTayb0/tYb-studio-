# Third-party notices

The first-phase web app uses browser-native HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Canvas, Service Worker, and WebView APIs. No third-party JavaScript libraries or AI models are bundled in this starter release.

The Android wrapper uses Android SDK APIs and the Android Gradle Plugin. Consult their respective license terms when building or redistributing the APK.

Before adding any library, codec, font, or AI model, verify its current license, redistribution terms, and attribution requirements.
